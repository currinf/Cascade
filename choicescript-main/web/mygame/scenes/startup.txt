Welcome to Cascade!

This is a demo of a decision-tree based scenario and its impacts on an ecosystem loosely inspired by 
the Bio Reserve at Denison University.  

*page_break

There is a stream near the community you live in. Recently, a colony of beavers have moved in and constructed 
dams along the stream channel. Vocal members of the community are now worried about potential flooding. You
could organize a team to clear out the beaver dams. 

*choice
  #Leave the beavers alone.
    You leave the beavers alone. Members of the community are unhappy with this choice.
    *set community -10
    *set dams true
    *finish
  #Remove the dams.
    Members of the community are happy the beavers are gone. Still, you wonder if this was the best
    choice long-term.
    *set community +10
    *set mammals +1
    *set dams false
    *finish
